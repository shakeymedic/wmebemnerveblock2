Safety Centre

This section centralizes all critical safety information, including the "Stop Before You Block" protocol and the management of Local Anaesthetic Systemic Toxicity (LAST). Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory for safe practice.

Stop Before You Block Checklist

This mandatory safety pause must be completed with an assistant (e.g., nurse) before every block.

Step 1: PREP (Led by Assistant)

Step 2: STOP (Operator & Assistant)

▶ Reconfirm: Block site matches consent form and site marking.

▶ Reconfirm: Block is appropriate for the indication.

Step 3: BLOCK (Operator & Assistant)

Proceed immediately with block. If an additional block is needed, return to Step 1: PREP.

Step 4: POST BLOCK (Assistant)

Restock and reseal block trolley/box.

LAST Management Protocol

This is a life-threatening emergency. The following is a summary of the AAGBI/RCoA guideline. A physical copy of the guideline and the LAST rescue kit must be immediately available.

SUSPECT LAST

Altered mental state, seizures, metallic taste, arrhythmias, cardiac arrest.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

  • STOP injecting LA.
  • CALL FOR HELP (announce "LAST Emergency").
  • Manage Airway, give 100% O2.
  • Ensure IV access & monitoring.

NO Circulatory Arrest

  • Control Seizures: IV Benzodiazepine (e.g., Midazolam 1-2mg).
  • Treat Hypotension/Bradycardia.
  • Consider Lipid Emulsion if instability persists.

Circulatory Arrest

  • Start High-Quality CPR.
  • Give Lipid Emulsion 20% IMMEDIATELY.
    • Bolus: 1.5 ml/kg over 2-3 mins.
    • Infusion: 15 ml/kg/hour.
  • MODIFY ALS: Use REDUCED Adrenaline doses (≤1 mcg/kg). AVOID Vasopressin.
  • Expect PROLONGED resuscitation.

AFTER THE EVENT

Transfer to monitored setting (HDU/ICU). Report via local incident system & MHRA Yellow Card.
